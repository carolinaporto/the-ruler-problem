# CONTENT SPEC — HAVE WE REACHED AGI?

Use this as the conceptual source of truth for the site.

## Core argument
The project should not answer “Have we reached AGI?” with a simple yes/no. It should show why the answer depends on the operational definition of intelligence and AGI.

The three lenses are:

### Psychology
Central question:
**Is there a general capacity underneath performance across tasks?**

Key concepts:
- g factor
- positive manifold
- fluid intelligence (Gf)
- crystallized intelligence (Gc)
- metacognition

Key visual relationship:
- Gc-like capabilities connect naturally to broad accumulated knowledge tests.
- Gf-like capabilities connect naturally to novel-problem-solving and transfer.
- This is an analogy for organizing evidence; do not claim psychometric equivalence between humans and AI.

### Evolution
Central question:
**What is intelligence for?**

Key concepts:
- adaptation under uncertainty
- novelty
- active exploration
- causal intervention
- inherited priors
- development
- culture
- cumulative knowledge

Key framing:
Human cognition does not start as a blank slate. Evolution and development provide structure; culture supplies accumulated knowledge.

The AI analogy:
architecture/design → pretraining → post-training → cultural data → new context → adaptation.

Always label:
**Analogy, not equivalence.**

The important synthesis:
**Both humans and AI systems enter novel situations with a history.**

### Computer Science
Central question:
**What can the system actually do, across how many domains, with what level of adaptation, reliability, and autonomy?**

Key dimensions:
- breadth
- performance
- novel learning/generalization
- autonomy
- reliability
- long-horizon capability

Key principle:
Do not collapse all of these into one fake AGI score.

# Main evidence nodes

## Levels of AGI
Use to explain:
- breadth / generality
- depth / performance
- autonomy as a separable deployment dimension

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02462

## Chollet — On the Measure of Intelligence
Use to explain:
- task skill is not identical to intelligence
- prior knowledge/training can “buy” task performance
- intelligence as skill-acquisition efficiency
- ARC as an attempt to emphasize generalization and fluid intelligence

Source:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547

## Humanity’s Last Exam
Use to show:
- breadth and expert-level academic reasoning/knowledge
- rapid frontier progress
- current benchmark evidence does not establish every dimension of AGI

Current source:
https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/humanitys_last_exam

As of the Sept. 17, 2026 update displayed on the source:
GPT-6 Astra: 54.80 ± 1.94 on HLE-Rolling.

Use exact date in tooltip/source detail if this number appears.

## ARC-AGI-3
Use to show:
- novel interactive environments
- no instructions/rules/stated goals
- exploration and adaptation
- system boundary/harness sensitivity

Sources:
https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-launch
https://arcprize.org/results/openai-gpt-6-astra
https://arcprize.org/blog/astra

Verified facts to use:

Use to show:

https://metr.org/time-horizons/

# Psychology and evolution evidence

## Spelke & Kinzler — Core Knowledge
Use:
Human cognition is proposed to be founded partly on systems for representing objects, actions, number, space, and potentially social partners.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-7687.2007.00569.x

## Goddu & Gopnik — causal learning
Use:
Children actively intervene in the world; human causal reasoning develops through more than passive observation.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-024-00300-5

## Cantlon & Piantadosi — expanded information capacity
Use:
One perspective argues that uniquely human cognition may arise partly from quantitative increases in global information-processing capacity and sharing across memory, attention, and learning, rather than only from many uniquely human domain-specific modules.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-024-00283-3

## Cultural intelligence hypothesis
Use:
Human distinctiveness may depend importantly on social-cognitive abilities that support participation in and learning from cultural groups.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17823346/

# Phrases to avoid

Avoid absolute, academically sloppy claims such as:
- “AI has achieved AGI.”
- “AI has not achieved AGI.”
- “ARC proves AGI.”
- “LLMs have human fluid intelligence.”
- “Evolution is pretraining.”
- “Culture is the same as training data.”
- “GPT has a g factor.”
- “Consciousness is required for AGI.”
- “Consciousness is irrelevant to AGI.”

Use:
- “conceptual analogy”
- “evidence for”
- “on this benchmark”
- “reported result”
- “does not by itself establish”
- “under this definition”
- “if this criterion is required”
