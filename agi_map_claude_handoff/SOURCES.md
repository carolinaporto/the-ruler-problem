## AGI definitions / measurement

### Morris et al. — Levels of AGI
Meredith Ringel Morris, Jascha Sohl-dickstein, Noah Fiedel, Tris Warkentin, Allan Dafoe, Aleksandra Faust, Clement Farabet, Shane Legg.
“Levels of AGI for Operationalizing Progress on the Path to AGI.”
https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02462

Use for:
- performance/depth
- generality/breadth
- autonomy as related but separable
- the problem of operationalizing AGI

### François Chollet — On the Measure of Intelligence
https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547

Use for:
- distinction between task skill and intelligence
- priors/training
- skill-acquisition efficiency
- ARC / fluid-intelligence-oriented evaluation
