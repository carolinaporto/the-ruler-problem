04 Lens 01 · Psychology

Psychology asks: is there something general underneath performance?

Concept · g factor

Does broad performance imply general intelligence?

Conceptual diagram — not data

In psychometrics, g is a statistical factor inferred from the positive correlations among performance on different cognitive tasks. It is not a single “AGI test.”

One implication for AI: broad success on one benchmark is less informative than a coherent pattern of capability across different kinds of tasks.

Not claimed here We have not established a g factor for AI systems. Read this as a question and an analogy, not a finding.

      12 Sources

      Where this comes from.

      Benchmark figures were checked against their source pages on Sept. 21, 2026. Every number on this page carries a link to where it was reported. Diagrams marked conceptual, illustrative, or analogy, not equivalence are interpretations built for this map, not measurements.

      How to read the claims

      • Reported means a figure a named source publishes on a named date. It is evidence “on this benchmark,” not proof of a construct.
      • Conceptual / illustrative marks positions and bar lengths that show relationships, not quantities.
      • Analogy, not equivalence marks every comparison between human cognition and AI systems.
      • Consciousness is treated as a separate, unresolved question that these benchmarks do not measure.
      • For g, fluid and crystallized intelligence, and the positive manifold, the map uses standard psychometrics framing with the background references above; nothing here claims a psychometric score for an AI system.
